Dental FAQ & Appointment Workflow

A candidate-provided n8n workflow for routing dental FAQ requests, validating appointment data, checking availability, and returning a structured result to a conversational agent.

  • Input validation for name, UAE mobile number, service, date, time, clinic hours, and booking windows.
  • Calendar conflict check before booking and documented cancellation/error paths.
  • Workflow export contains 82 nodes and 55 connection groups; no production claim is made.
